📜 የኮንከር 14ቱ ህጎች (Conquer Rules)
  1. Yemejemeria ye meret: Starter's first ground discard cannot be drawn even with 41 points.
  2. Joker Naming: The player who joins cuts/names the round's Joker.
  3. Golden Star (⭐): Designated Jokers display a gold star badge.
  4. 41 + Tris: Need ≥ 41 points AND at least 1 valid Tris to pick from the ground.
  5. ተጣይ: Discard 14th card to ground after melding 13 cards to conquer.
  6. Joker Drop: If a Joker is accidentally discarded, opponent can pick it up.
  7. Endless Deck: Reshuffle ground cards when deck empties.
  8. Poke & 30s: Poke opponent—if they don't play in 30s, you win.
  9. Joker Swap: Real cards can replace table Jokers to take Joker back into hand.
  10. 4 Aces: Quatris of Aces equals 41 points and unlocks ground draw.
  11. J-Q-K-A: Consecutive J-Q-K-A suited counts as 41 points.
  12. Base Card Win: Using Base card allows winning without revealing early melds.
  13. Joker Steal: Replace table Joker to complete your conquer.
  14. 1 Joker Limit: Maximum 1 Joker per Tris or Quatris.
